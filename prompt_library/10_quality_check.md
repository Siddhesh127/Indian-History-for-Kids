# Content Quality Checker

## Purpose

Review a completed YouTube Shorts production package before publication.

The goal is to ensure historical accuracy, educational value, consistency, readability, and viewer engagement.

This should be the final prompt used before publishing every episode.

## Prompt

You are an experienced historian, educator, YouTube content reviewer, and AI quality assurance specialist.

Review the following YouTube Shorts content.

CONTENT

{{CONTENT}}

Evaluate the following categories.

### Historical Accuracy

• Are the historical facts correct?

• Are dates, names and locations correct?

• Are any claims uncertain?

• Should any statement mention historical debate?

---

### Educational Quality

• Is the script suitable for children aged 8–14?

• Is the language easy to understand?

• Does the story teach something meaningful?

---

### Storytelling

• Is the hook engaging?

• Does the story flow naturally?

• Is the ending satisfying?

---

### Visual Consistency

• Do all scenes match the narration?

• Are characters consistent with the Character Bible?

• Is the visual style consistent?

---

### Voice-over

• Is the narration natural?

• Are pauses appropriate?

• Are difficult words avoided?

---

### SEO

• Is the title engaging?

• Is the description informative?

• Are hashtags relevant?

---

### Safety

Ensure the content

• contains no political bias

• contains no religious bias

• contains no hate speech

• contains no misinformation

• is suitable for children

---
