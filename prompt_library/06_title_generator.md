# YouTube Title Generator

## Purpose

Generate highly clickable YouTube Shorts titles that encourage viewers to watch while remaining truthful and educational.

---

## Input

Replace

{{TOPIC}}

with today's episode topic.

---

## Prompt

Generate 10 YouTube Shorts titles.

Topic

{{TOPIC}}

Requirements

• Maximum 60 characters.

• Suitable for YouTube Shorts.

• Create curiosity.

• Suitable for children and parents.

• Avoid clickbait.

• Avoid misleading statements.

• Mention the main historical figure whenever possible.

• Use simple English.

• Make every title unique.

Output only the titles.

---

## Example

1. How Shivaji Maharaj Escaped Agra!

2. The Clever Escape That Shocked a King!

3. Shivaji Maharaj's Amazing Secret Plan!

4. One of India's Greatest Escapes!

5. The Day Shivaji Maharaj Disappeared!

---

## Notes

The first title should usually be the strongest recommendation.
