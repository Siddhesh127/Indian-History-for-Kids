# Character Bible

## Purpose

Maintain a consistent appearance for every historical figure throughout the entire "Indian History for Kids" series.

Whenever an AI image prompt includes a character listed below, always use the same description.

Never change hairstyles, facial features, clothing, colors, or accessories unless the historical event specifically requires it.

---

# Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj

Role

Founder of the Maratha Empire

Appearance

• Adult male (approximately mid-30s to early-40s)
• Warm brown eyes
• Oval face
• Neatly trimmed black beard and moustache
• Calm, confident and courageous expression

Clothing

• Bright saffron turban with jeweled ornament
• White royal robes
• Gold chest armor
• Pearl necklace
• Traditional Maratha sword
• Brown leather boots

Personality

• Brave
• Wise
• Calm under pressure
• Inspirational leader

Always maintain:

• Character appearance
• Facial features
• Clothing colors
• Jewelry
• Weapons

Future characters should be added using the same format.
